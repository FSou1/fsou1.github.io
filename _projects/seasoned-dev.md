As part of my project, SeasonedDev, I created a YouTube and Telegram channels where I shared my knowledge with developers.

On my YouTube channel, I recorded and uploaded videos on topics related to React, [Node.js](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jma5FVjjxMk), and [Deno]({% post_url 2022-01-06-Build_a_graphql_server_with_deno %}), as well as coding projects like [how to parse Google Maps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xOD4-M2jSw). I also shared tips and tricks that could help developers improve their skills and become more effective programmers.

In addition to my YouTube channel, I also created a Telegram channel where I posted notes about programming. These notes covered a wide range of topics. I helped my audience become better programmers and stay updated with the latest trends and technologies by sharing insights and advice.
