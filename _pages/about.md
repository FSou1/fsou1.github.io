I'm Maxim Zhukov, a Staff Software Engineer and Frontend Architect at PayPal, based in Houston, TX. I have been building software since 2008, first on .NET and SQL Server, and for the last several years mostly with React, TypeScript, and Node.js.

## What I do now

At PayPal I'm the tech lead on the team that owns the React component platform behind PayPal.com and Venmo.com. That is more than 6,500 pages and 4.8M daily views. I lead its architecture, standards, and technical direction.

Some of the work I'm most proud of:

- Improved the PayPal.com homepage LCP by 36% on desktop (3.48s → 2.24s) and 46% on mobile (4.24s → 2.29s).
- Led the migration of Venmo.com from a static site to the component platform, covering 260+ marketing and legal pages.
- Built the design token and multi-brand theming pipeline (Tokens Studio → Style Dictionary) used for the Oslo rebrand across 5,000+ pages and 201 markets.
- Moved the component library's CI/CD from a monolithic Jenkins pipeline to a package-agnostic Turborepo monorepo.
- Made visual regression testing reliable at about 3,400 Playwright screenshots. I wrote about it in [Earning Trust in Visual Tests]({% post_url 2026-11-12-Visual_testing_at_paypal %}).

The full story is on the [PayPal experience page](/experience/2023-paypal/).
